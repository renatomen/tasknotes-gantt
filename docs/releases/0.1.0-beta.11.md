# TaskNotes Gantt 0.1.0-beta.11

The largest release so far, and the first since July: working-time calendars arrive as a complete feature — calendar notes, a visual editor, working patterns, availability, and calendar sets — alongside a context-aware legend, independent bar treatment channels, and TaskNotes calendar items rendered directly in the timeline. It also carries an important date-handling fix for anyone outside UTC.

## Added

- **Calendars and working time.** (#267, #268, #269, #270, #271, #272, #273, #275, #276, #277, #278, #279, #281)
  - The Gantt now understands *when work actually happens*. A calendar is an ordinary note in your vault describing a working pattern — which days and which hours count as working time — and the chart reads it: non-working days are drawn as background shading, and a bar whose span comes from an effort estimate stretches over them instead of pretending weekends are workdays.
  - Calendars combine into **calendar sets**, so a team calendar, a personal one, and a holiday feed can apply together. Where two calendars disagree, the chart surfaces the conflict rather than silently picking one, and a status banner tells you when a calendar could not be resolved.
  - Pick calendars with the **Select calendars** command and its picker modal, see a calendar's markers and a today line over the timeline, and optionally use the calendar as a bar colour source so each task carries the colour of the calendar it belongs to.
  - Working patterns are described with standard iCalendar recurrence rules (RFC 5545), so the same calendar means the same thing to any other tool that reads the note.
- **A visual editor for calendar notes.** (#286, #287, #288, #289, #290, #291, #292, #293, #294, #295, #296, #297, #298, #300, #301, #302, #303, #304, #305)
  - Open a calendar note and edit it as a form instead of by hand: a working-pattern builder for the recurrence rules, a searchable timezone picker showing live UTC offsets, and a collapsible colour picker accepting CSS colour names or hex.
  - Three preview tabs show the pattern you are building before you commit to it — a **year grid**, a **week** view, and a **Gantt strip** — and conflicts between calendars in a set are explained on hover.
  - The editor keeps a sticky header with its tabs, Save button, and an unsaved-changes cue, warns before you close a note with unsaved edits, and preserves the comments already in your note's frontmatter.
  - New **Create calendar** and **Create calendar set** commands start a note from a template, and a calendar can be renamed from a Name field in the editor.
- **Availability blocks — one-off exceptions to a working pattern.** (#308, #321)
  - A calendar can carry specific dated blocks on top of its recurring pattern: a holiday, a training day, a week away. Blocks combine with the top-level pattern rather than replacing it, so a normal working week with three days blocked renders as exactly that.
- **TaskNotes calendar items in the timeline.** (#386)
  - The Gantt can render what the TaskNotes calendar shows: recurring task instances, time entries, timeblocks, property-based events, and external calendars (ICS, Google, Microsoft). Recurring tasks render as one authored row with occupancy pieces beneath it, so a series stays readable when zoomed out.
  - Per-view toggles mirror the TaskNotes calendar's own, and a source switcher hides or restores any contributing source for the current opening without changing your saved settings. External calendars reuse TaskNotes' cached data and start no network fetches of their own. Calendar items are read-only — your task bars stay the single editable representation of a task's dates.
- **A context-aware interactive legend.** (#391, #406)
  - Every chart can now explain itself. The legend samples the view you are actually looking at — its theme, palettes, treatment choices, calendar colour, estimate meaning, and non-working-time rendering — and covers task, date, calendar, dependency, occurrence, estimate, and per-task override semantics.
  - It overlays the chart at the right or bottom and can be moved live for the current opening; the Appearance setting chooses the default position for future openings. The chart stays mounted throughout, so geometry and selection survive opening, moving, and closing it.
  - ![The legend docked to the right of a Gantt chart, listing the meaning of each bar colour, hatch, and marker in the current view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/renatomen/tasknotes-gantt/0.1.0-beta.11/docs/media/gantt-legend-right.png)
  - ![The same legend docked along the bottom of the chart, scrolling horizontally](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/renatomen/tasknotes-gantt/0.1.0-beta.11/docs/media/gantt-legend-bottom.png)
- **Independent Fill, Strip, and Icon treatment channels.** (#312)
  - Bar fill colour, the left-edge colour strip, and the on-bar icon are now chosen separately. Colour bars by status while striping by priority and showing a project icon — previously these competed for one setting.
- **Estimate meaning is its own setting, separate from how non-working days are drawn.** (#315, #319, #320)
  - Two questions that used to share one control are now separate. **Estimate meaning** decides whether an effort estimate means working time (re-projected across working days) or elapsed calendar time. **Non-working-day rendering** decides whether blocked days appear as background shading or as dimmed segments inside the bar. Every combination is now reachable, including elapsed-time dates with the non-working days still drawn in.
  - Estimate meaning has a per-view default and a **per-task override** property. An overridden task carries a small accent dot on its upper-left corner, with a halo so it stays visible over any bar colour, and hovering it explains the interpretation.
  - Authored dates are never moved by either setting — only dates derived from an estimate re-project.
- **A prompt when you drag a task whose dates are inferred.** (#314, #336, #347, #348)
  - Dragging a bar whose span comes from an estimate rather than authored dates now asks what you meant: grow the estimate, or write real dates to the note. The answer applies immediately and can be remembered for the rest of the session.
- **A torn edge marks a date the task does not actually have.** (#395, #397, #398, #406)
  - When one end of a bar is inferred rather than written down, that end is cut with a zigzag edge instead of a flat one, so an inferred boundary never looks authored. It appears on its own where only one end is non-authored, and the legend explains it.
  - ![A Gantt bar with a torn zigzag edge on its right end, marking a date that was inferred rather than authored](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/renatomen/tasknotes-gantt/0.1.0-beta.11/docs/media/missing-date-torn-edge.png)

## Changed

- **Leaving a field mapping blank now means "use the TaskNotes default", not "no property".** (#254)
  - A blank *Status Property* previously switched off everything that depended on status: colouring bars by status did nothing, the status icon did nothing, and double-clicking a status cell opened the note instead of the picker. Blank mappings now resolve to the properties TaskNotes is configured with, for status and priority as well as dates, and inline editors resolve against the same values.

## Removed

- **The red border on tasks with swapped dates.** (#412)
  - A task whose start falls after its due date no longer draws a red outline. The orange fill still marks it. A fuller treatment — a badge beside the bar naming what is wrong — is planned; until it ships, a swapped task shown with split rendering carries no visible cue.

## Fixed

- **Tasks no longer shift by a day, and stay put after you drag them.** (#489, #490)
  - Dates written as plain calendar days were read as UTC instants, so in any timezone behind UTC a task saved as September 21–23 drew on September 20–22 — and dragging it then saved the wrong day, compounding with every correction. Calendar dates are now read as calendar dates.
  - Three further defects found while verifying the fix are also closed: a moved three-day bar could briefly display as two days, closely spaced corrective moves could roll back after a refresh, and resizing a task that appears in more than one place could leave the other copy behind.
  - Thanks to @donaldwdci for reporting this and for the detailed follow-up that made the remaining causes findable.
- **Tooltips never appeared on touch-capable hardware.** (#404)
  - Any device reporting a touch point — including most laptops with a touchscreen — suppressed every tooltip in the chart. They now render regardless.
- **Two Gantt views in one workspace no longer interfere with each other.** (#316)
  - Injected styles were shared across views, so opening a second chart could restyle the first. Each chart now owns its own.
- **Hiding top-level tasks and date-status colouring now follow the current chart state.** (#472, #473)
  - A task nested under a new parent during a refresh could stay visible with *Hide top-level* on, and date-status colouring could paint from stale state.
- **Icons on one-cell bars are aligned.** (#393)
- **A live Bar Fill change no longer garbles split rendering.** (#318)
- **The legend's swatches match the chart.** (#402, #407, #410)
  - The torn-edge swatch now shows the same cut the chart draws, shading swatches have cells to contrast against, and the legend no longer describes a treatment that was retired.
- **Calendar editing and validation.** (#299, #302, #323, #324, #326, #327, #328, #329, #330, #331, #333, #334, #335, #337, #338)
  - Impossible dates and malformed or unbounded recurrence rules are rejected before they can freeze or mis-render the preview; a colour is sanitised before it reaches an inline style; Discard is disabled while a save is in flight; a newly created calendar note routes to the editor even when the vault indexes it late; deleting a calendar note re-resolves the chart; folder-qualified calendar links resolve; and the conflict banner attributes each conflict to the calendar it came from.
